PIMS-CRG (2026–2029)
PIMS Collaborative Research Group

Asymptotic Geometric Analysis, Convex and Discrete Geometry, and Combinatorics

This PIMS Collaborative Research Group (2026–2029) brings together researchers in asymptotic geometric analysis, convex and discrete geometry, and combinatorics to study the structure of high-dimensional convex bodies, geometric and functional inequalities, and the combinatorial phenomena they give rise to. The group spans the University of Calgary, the University of Alberta, the University of Manitoba, and the University of Washington.
